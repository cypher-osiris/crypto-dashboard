  4. Buy ZBCN on the spot market (ZBCN/USD). A limit order at a level you chose (see the support levels above) avoids paying the spread on a market order.
  5. Decide where to keep it. For long-term holdings, consider moving it to your own wallet (below). When you withdraw, pick the Zebec Network network your wallet supports: sending on the wrong network can lose the funds.

Self-custody: keeping ZBCN in your own wallet

Keep long-term holdings in a hardware wallet like Ledger. Coins on an exchange are an IOU from that exchange. A hardware wallet keeps the private keys on a small offline device, so you approve every transfer yourself. Before you send ZBCN to any wallet, check that it supports the Zebec Network network, write the recovery phrase on paper (never in a photo or a cloud note), and send a small test amount first.
