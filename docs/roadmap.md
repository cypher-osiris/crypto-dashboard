# Lumi Trades: growth roadmap

## Guardrails for the nightly job
- One or two focused changes per night, always shippable; never break `data/*.json` contracts or the publish.sh / track_trades.py / signals.py jobs.
- No account sign-ups, payments, outreach or X posts without the owner's explicit approval. Draft them instead.
- Every number or claim on a new page must come from the data files or a verified source.
- Generated files (coin pages, `trades/`, `sitemap.xml`, the marked blocks in index.html) are rewritten by build_pages.py: change the generator, not the output.
