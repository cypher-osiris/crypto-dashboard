Buy / sell signals

What a signal is: a mechanical score from RSI, MACD, moving averages (EMA), Bollinger Bands, StochRSI and volume on 1h, 4h and 1d candles (weighted 25 / 35 / 40%). They are indicator readings, not predictions.

Honest note: Lumi's backtest found no reliable edge in these signals on their own. Lumi uses them as one input next to levels, news and the BTC backdrop.
