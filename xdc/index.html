How to buy XDC (XDC Network)

In the US

  • Kraken (XDC/USD)
  • Uphold (XDC)

Outside the US

  • A supported exchange (XDC/USDT spot)
  • Kraken in many countries
  1. Pick an exchange that lists XDC where you live. Coinbase doesn't list XDC, so US buyers use Kraken or Uphold.
  2. Create an account and verify your identity. Regulated exchanges ask for ID and proof of address before you can deposit.
  3. Fund it. A bank transfer (ACH/SEPA) is usually the cheapest; cards cost more.
  4. Buy XDC on the spot market (XDC/USD). A limit order at a level you chose (see the support levels above) avoids paying the spread on a market order.
  5. Decide where to keep it. For long-term holdings, consider moving it to your own wallet (below). When you withdraw, pick the XDC Network network your wallet supports: sending on the wrong network can lose the funds.

Self-custody: keeping XDC in your own wallet

Keep long-term holdings in a hardware wallet like Ledger. Coins on an exchange are an IOU from that exchange. A hardware wallet keeps the private keys on a small offline device, so you approve every transfer yourself. Before you send XDC to any wallet, check that it supports the XDC Network network, write the recovery phrase on paper (never in a photo or a cloud note), and send a small test amount first.
