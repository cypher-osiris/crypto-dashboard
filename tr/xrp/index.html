Network: the XRPL fixCleanup3_4_0 security fix will no longer activate Oct 23; validator support briefly dipped to 26 of 35, which reset the 14-day clock, and it's now slated for Nov 4 with 31 of 35 votes (CoinDesk). Policy: the OCC's final GENIUS Act rule (effective Jan 18, 2027) keeps stablecoin rewards on a case-by-case basis, and the American Bankers Association pushed back on that (Roll Call); that's a months-long story for RLUSD, not a price driver today.

No new idea: a long at $1.33 with a stop under $1.29 has only ~1:1 to the EMA cluster, and a short into $1.30-1.33 support has no room.
