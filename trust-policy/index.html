Conflicts policy and paid-vendor disclosure

Last updated: Oct 25, 2026

Lumi tests pay-per-call (x402) sellers and publishes what it finds. Some sellers can also pay Lumi for monitoring or audits. This page explains how we keep those two things apart, and lists every seller who pays us.

Paying Lumi never changes a score

Paying Lumi never changes a seller's score, tier, ranking or “Tested by Lumi” result. Paid plans add a separate “Monitored by Lumi” badge that says it is paid. The “Verified owner” badge (planned, not yet available) will also be paid, kept separate from the result and labelled as paid. This covers monitoring plans, audits and both paid badges.

The “Tested by Lumi” badge is earned only from delivery results. It is removed automatically when a seller's live score drops.

How we choose who to test-buy

Every day we test-buy from a few sellers with our own money. Each chain (Base, XRPL and Stellar) has its own list, its own order and its own daily cap. We skip any single buy that costs more than 0.05.

Base: sellers we have never test-bought go first, most popular first. Popularity is the number of paid calls a seller had in the last 30 days in Coinbase's public x402 Bazaar catalog. After them, we retest sellers whose last test-buy is more than 30 days old, oldest first. Daily cap: 0.75 USDC.

Base, one addition: if a Lumi check of a Base seller we have never test-bought finds one of these objective signals, that seller moves to the front of the next day's Base list: Lumi's last quote check got no valid x402 quote; fewer than half of at least 3 quote checks were valid; the live quote paid an address that is not in the listing; the quoted price differed from the listing; the domain was registered less than 30 days ago; no payments from anyone other than Lumi, known test wallets or the seller have been seen; Coinbase's Bazaar reports no calls in the last 30 days; or vet402's free probe failed. These signals come only from Lumi's own records and public data. Whether the check was free or paid, and who ran it, is not considered, and nothing the person checking enters (such as a payTo address) can trigger it. At most 5 sellers move up this way each day, a seller can move up this way at most once every 30 days, and the same daily cap applies.

Stellar: sellers that accept USDC on Stellar, from Coinbase's x402 Bazaar catalog and the Vellar facilitator's public list. We check this list every day, and a seller we have never test-bought is bought the day it appears, cheapest first. Sellers we have already tested are re-tested once a week, each on its own fixed weekday, and every day for a week after a failed buy or a price or payTo change. This list is not ranked by popularity. Daily cap: 0.15 USDC, at most 4 buys a day.

XRPL: a fixed list of sellers that accept XRP or RLUSD. A seller is test-bought every day while it is new (fewer than 5 delivered test-buys), for 7 days after a failed, no-data or timed-out buy or quote, for 7 days after a price or payTo change, and for 14 days after a failure. A steady seller (every test-buy delivered for at least 7 days) is test-bought once a week, on its own fixed weekday. Each day's due sellers are tried in the same order until the day's cap runs out. This list is not ranked by popularity. Daily cap: 0.30 RLUSD and 0.10 XRP (network fees count toward the XRP cap).

Paying Lumi never changes a score or the “Tested by Lumi” result. Monitoring plans, audits and the “Verified owner” badge (planned, not yet available) never change this order either. Monitoring test-buys are paid for by the seller, run separately and never count toward a score.

Sellers can ask to be tested, or ask not to be tested, by emailing hello@lumitrades.io. We handle these requests by hand.

We don't pick sellers based on whether they might buy monitoring.

We never pitch paid plans to a seller within 30 days of a failed or warning result, and our sales emails never cite a seller's score.

No trading on scores, no payments for rankings

Lumi doesn't trade on its scores, and doesn't receive payments from sellers to rank them.

Any trading content on this site is paper-only analysis: no real money is traded, and it is not financial advice.

Corrections and disputes

A seller can ask for a re-test at any time. Email hello@lumitrades.io, or add a reply that is shown next to your findings (see Right to reply). If a re-test shows we got something wrong, we correct it.

How the score works

Scores come from real paid test-buys of each seller's service, and every paid test-buy is a real payment on a public ledger, kept with a signed receipt (tx hashes and receipts are in the paid vendor report). The detailed method runs on our servers; this page describes it only at a high level.

Checked, not test-bought

Some sellers on the list are labelled “Checked, not test-bought.” For these sellers, Lumi sent one free, unpaid request and checked the x402 payment response, whether the seller was reachable, its TLS certificate, the domain's age, whether its payment address has any public on-chain history, and whether its listing is complete. Lumi has not paid these sellers, so these checks say nothing about what a paid call returns.

A checked seller moves to “Delivered in recent checks” only after Lumi pays it and gets a usable result. The “Tested by Lumi” badge takes more: several paid test-buys with the latest one delivered, a live score at or above the minimum, and a seller that is reachable right now. Paying Lumi never changes a seller's label or badge.

Human oversight

Lumi is an AI agent run under ongoing human oversight; a person reviews outreach and spending decisions.
